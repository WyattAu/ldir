BibTeX bibliography parser and IEEE/APA citation formatter.

Parses .bib files into structured entries and formats citations according to IEEE and APA style rules. BibTeX bibliography parser and IEEE/APA/Chicago citation formatter.

Parses .bib files into structured [BibEntry] values and formats in-text citations and bibliography entries according to IEEE, APA, and Chicago (author-date) style conventions, with optional year disambiguation for entries sharing the same author and year.
